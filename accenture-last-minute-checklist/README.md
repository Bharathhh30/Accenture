## What is most evidenced for the general India fresher technical MCQ

Accenture does not publish one universal MCQ syllabus; its official recruiting information describes assessments as role-dependent. A recent public candidate account names **pseudocode, DSA, DBMS, cloud, networks, and MS Office** as MCQ areas but does not provide a verified question list. A 2026 preparation-provider blueprint also emphasizes **Office, pseudocode (including OOP/DS/DBMS/C basics), and network security/cloud**. Therefore:

- **Higher evidence for the commonly described campus MCQ:** pseudocode, networking, cloud, security basics, Office/Excel, DBMS.
- **Useful core-CS or role-dependent revision:** operating systems.
- **More role/job-description dependent than established general MCQ sections:** DevOps and SDLC; these are more strongly associated with software delivery/project knowledge and role-specific interviews than with the published general MCQ outline.

“Higher evidence” means multiple public descriptions mention the area, **not** that a specific question is guaranteed. Prioritize the assessment invitation and job description over any third-party syllabus.
